## Rol y alcance

Amazonia Impact Ventures is an impact-first investment company dedicated to providing finance directly to sustainable enterprises in the Amazon rainforest. We empower Indigenous peoples and local communities in South America to protect and regenerate their forests and biodiversity and improve their livelihoods while harvesting local forest products, such as acai, Brazil nuts, coffee and cacao.
