## Elevator Pitch

Salvadoran startup developing machine-learning-based technology for sustainable agriculture. Agrismart offers a smart crop monitoring system starting at about $300 per system to help farmers save water and improve productivity in greenhouses, nurseries, and commercial farms.

## Climate Problem

Irrigation and input use drive cost and water stress for horticulture producers. Smallholders and commercial growers need affordable monitoring to use water more efficiently.

## Solution

Modular, low-cost smart monitoring: environmental sensors combined in one dashboard, local data processing with sync when connectivity is available, and actionable insights for precision irrigation and crop management.

## Revenue Model

Sale/installation of monitoring systems (deck cites starting at $300 per system). Deck beachhead modeling references scaling to 3,522 deployed systems at 20% penetration in El Salvador smallholder horticulture (Year 3 scenario in deck).

## Target Market

Beachhead: smallholder horticulture in El Salvador, plus nurseries and greenhouses. Long-term deck segmentation references broader Central America smallholder agriculture after beachhead validation.
