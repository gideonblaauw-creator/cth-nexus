## Elevator Pitch

PezConfiable turns scattered information into useful decisions for artisanal fishers — combining sea conditions, prices, regulations, traceability, and commercial connection into one fishery information and traceability system.

## Climate Problem

Artisanal fishers lack timely, usable information (ocean conditions, prices, rules, and market access), which weakens resilience and fair value capture in the fishery value chain. *(Deck cites FAO et al., 2023 and PRODUCE as market sources — verify citations on publish.)*

## Solution

A system that supports the fisherman before, during, and after every fishing trip, turning his information into visibility and new opportunities — from scattered information to better decisions at sea and in the market.
