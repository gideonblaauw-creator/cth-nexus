## Elevator Pitch

Andean Biofactories delivers community biofactories as a service—cultivating food, health, and local independence—sold to mining-sector clients funding social investment and ESG reporting.

## Climate Problem

High-altitude communities face costly protein access, carbohydrate-heavy diets, and anemia prevalence. Fragile supply chains and social investment programs seek measurable, sustained local nutrition outcomes.

## Solution

A modular, automated community biofactory model producing food locally, with ongoing accompaniment to build local production capacity and food sovereignty.
