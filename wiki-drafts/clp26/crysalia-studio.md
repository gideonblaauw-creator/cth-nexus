## Elevator Pitch

Crysalia Studio—transformed goods with purpose. Furniture and décor design pieces made from aesthetically rejected wood for decorators, designers, specialized stores, and real-estate developers.

## Climate Problem

Rejected or underutilized wood in Guatemala’s furniture supply chain is wasted or burned instead of staying in productive use. Deck frames imported wooden products (~$291M TAM reference in deck) vs local valorization opportunity.

## Solution

Design and material engineering to turn rejected timber into decoration and furniture products, extending carbon stored in wood in functional pieces (deck cites ~84 kg CO₂ sequestered per piece on average).
