## Elevator Pitch

Private capital will not move without reliable climate information. CLIMATIK delivers climate intelligence for manufacturers—connecting operational data to climate and financial KPIs without replacing core ERP systems.

## Climate Problem

Manufacturers need credible, audit-ready climate metrics for lenders and investors, but lack integrated tools that translate operations into GHG Protocol–aligned indicators at acceptable cost and risk.

## Solution

SaaS climate intelligence: connects to existing operational data (e.g. SAP Business One, Dynamics 365), calculates Scope 1–2 footprints, PCAF-aligned scores, and localized emission factors under GHG Protocol methodologies—quality and energy indicators in near real time.

## Revenue Model

Annual SaaS subscription; deck cites weighted average price on the order of **USD 13,286 / year** (verify packaging and currency in deck).

## Target Market

Manufacturing companies needing climate KPIs and disclosure-ready outputs for banks and ESG confidence. Segmentation details on deck market slides — expand from deck only.
