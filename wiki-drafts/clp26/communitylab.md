## Elevator Pitch

From waste to resource: communitylab turns factory waste data into profit through the circular economy—software and sensors that help plants measure residues and monetize them via **SICircular**.

## Climate Problem

Industrial residues are lost value and drive emissions when flows are invisible. Informality in circular networks limits traceability, compliance, and environmental performance.

## Solution

**Sistema Integral Circular (SICircular):** modular analytics software plus sensors and cameras to quantify waste streams, trace materials, connect industrial symbiosis opportunities, and produce auditable environmental indicators.

## Revenue Model

B2B platform sale; deck cites verified share-of-market economics around **~USD 16,238/year per client** and year-3 revenue scenario **USD 1,694,250** (service cost still to validate per deck).

## Target Market
