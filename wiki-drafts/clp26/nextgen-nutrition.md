## Elevator Pitch

Engineering the future of animal nutrition with **high-performance, low-carbon ingredients**. **0% meat**, powered by **insects, plants and natural bioactives** — functional precision protein cores, microbiome and palatability solutions for formulators.

## Climate Problem

Deck frames pet food reliance on livestock-derived protein and food side-stream waste; positions bioconversion and formulation to reduce carbon intensity vs. meat-based ingredients.

## Solution

Transform revalorized side streams and insect/plant inputs into functional ingredients. First application via **PetFly**: functional, hypoallergenic supplements for sensitive pets. Ingredient offer cited at **USD 490 / kg** (deck).

## Revenue Model

B2B ingredient sales (**USD 490 / kg** in deck). PetFly go-to-market via supplements; live wiki adds B2B2C/D2C pricing (~USD 70 / 12-pack) — **FLAG:** confirm which figures to surface on Idea Browser at HITL.

Global pet nutrition formulators; beachhead **sensitive/allergic dogs** in Colombia. Deck: reach **USD 18M ARR** by capturing **3% (~49K)** of **1.7M** allergic house dogs (from **8.2M** total) — assumptions stated in deck.
