## Elevator Pitch

Águilas Verdes Farm offers chemical-free meat from cattle raised with high welfare and environmentally responsible production, positioning regenerative livestock as climate-positive agriculture.

## Climate Problem

Conventional cattle production can rely on chemicals and miss carbon sequestration on pasture. Deck cites sequestration of over 4 tons of CO₂ per head annually under their regenerative model.

## Solution

Regenerative, chemical-free cattle farming with improved organic matter, carbon capture on pasture, and cost savings from eliminating agrochemicals (deck cites >50% chemical-cost savings framing and ~$7.5M/year scenario line—verify in deck PDF).

## Revenue Model

Sale of live animals or carcasses direct to slaughterhouses, butcher shops, restaurants, and consumers; deck emphasizes cutting middlemen and nutrition-efficient genetics.

## Target Market

Nationwide meat consumers in Costa Rica including restaurants, butcher shops, and slaughterhouses (deck cites large consumer base figures; OCR quality poor—confirm in deck).
