## Elevator Pitch

From fire to fiber: Piña & Kakaw sells sustainable pineapple-fiber blended yarn and fabric to fashion brands and textile manufacturers at competitive prices—upcycling agricultural waste instead of burning leaves.

## Climate Problem

Burning pineapple leaves releases emissions and wastes biomass; fossil-based textiles dominate today’s industry. Deck contrasts fossil-based textiles with renewable, breathable natural fibers.

## Solution

Pineapple + cotton blended yarn for fashion collections; jersey knit pineapple + cotton blend fabric for producers—natural, breathable materials from crop residue.

## Revenue Model

Yarn about $14/kg and fabric about $7/kg in deck pricing slide (labels partially garbled in OCR—confirm units in PDF). B2B sales to brands and manufacturers; pre-order stage per program metadata.
