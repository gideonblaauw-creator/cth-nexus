## Elevator Pitch

Own the ride. Earn the difference. SmartDrive revolutionizes electric mobility in Central America with a rent-to-own platform: one Kaiyi electric vehicle (~400 km range), ready day one, for ride-hail and delivery drivers on Uber, InDrive, and similar platforms.

## Climate Problem

Combustion vehicles used in ride-hail and delivery generate CO₂ and high fuel share of driver income. Deck cites ~38% of income to gasoline vs ~4% energy cost on EV operation in their comparison.

## Solution

SmartDrive provides the EV and a fixed weekly payment model (deck cites $210/week) so drivers earn while paying a simple rate. Platform bundles vehicle access with the operating model described in deck materials.

## Revenue Model

Weekly rent-to-own payments from drivers (deck: $210/week). Deck also references rental market sizing for digital mobility platforms in Central America.

## Target Market

Beachhead: full-time ride-hail/delivery drivers in El Salvador (deck cites ~3,100 full-time drivers nationally, ~1,000 in San Salvador metropolitan area). Broader Central America rental market figures appear in deck market slides.
