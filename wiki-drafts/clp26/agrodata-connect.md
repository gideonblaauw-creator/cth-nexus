## Elevator Pitch

AgroData Connect helps smallholder farmers in Colombia make data-driven agricultural decisions instead of relying on guesswork. The platform uses artificial intelligence so small farmers are less likely to lose their harvest.

## Climate Problem

Small producers face high production losses; the deck states that loss in production is the primary pain point and that producers know what those losses cost them.

## Solution

AI agricultural platform delivered via app, web, and WhatsApp. Associations can give members access and crop diagnostics in seconds. Validation includes association technicians reviewing alerts to build trust in diagnoses.

## Revenue Model

B2B licences sold to producer associations and federations in Colombia: **USD 8 per month per licence** (example in deck: 300 licences → **USD 28,800 per year** as a recurring association fee, with member access at no extra cost).

## Target Market

Initial beachhead: potato grower associations (deck cites a **USD 0.43M** corridor opportunity for 2027). Expansion path in deck: Colombia federations (~1.9M producers), then Latin America (~14M small producers).
