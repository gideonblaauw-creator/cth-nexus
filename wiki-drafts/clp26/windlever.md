## Elevator Pitch

WindLever converts wind energy into decisions for your crop — bladeless wind generation plus environmental and soil sensing, with data delivery for agro-export monitoring.

## Climate Problem

Agro-export fields need autonomous monitoring, but powering distributed sensors and turning field data into decisions remains costly and fragmented.

## Solution

Bladeless aerogenerator (**aerogenerador sin palas**), environmental sensor, and soil data — integrated with IoT sensors and AI agents to automate agricultural decisions (deck: “produce more with less”). Self-recharging sensor concept per deck.

## Revenue Model

Commercial offer (deck excerpt): **US$100** one-time + **US$1,000/year** subscription per **10 hectares** for data and monitoring (“pago por DATOS y MONITOREO”) — verify packaging before publish.
