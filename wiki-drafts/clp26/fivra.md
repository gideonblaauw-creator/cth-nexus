## Elevator Pitch

FIVRA is a platform that generates AI-developed textile technical spec sheets to cut production waste—starting from about US$4 per sheet—with packages (Starter, Basic, Pro) for different volumes.

## Climate Problem

Textile production waste and rework from incomplete specs drive unnecessary sampling, fabric use, and remakes before garments reach market.

## Solution

Each generated sheet includes full technical specifications, AI-generated back views, production risk analysis, sustainable material suggestions, and fabric waste optimization (deck example: “Vestido Catalina”).

## Revenue Model

Direct sale of spec-sheet packages to entrepreneurs and emerging brands; tiered Starter / Basic / Pro pricing on the platform.

## Target Market

Beachhead: agile textile entrepreneurs in LATAM; Peru deck cites ~46,693 companies (Statista 2026) and Lima entrepreneur counts (MINTRA). LATAM market size figures appear in deck (Statista 2024).

## Competitors
