## Elevator Pitch

AgroLens offers an integral, recurrent agricultural monitoring service so growers can see where to act before losses spread. The stack combines drone, on-farm station, and a web platform for ongoing field visibility.

## Climate Problem

Heat, water deficit, and crop stress often show up late when monitoring is manual or infrequent. Agroexport growers report that late detection leaves less time to respond across the farm.

## Solution

AgroLens installs, operates, and maintains monitoring hardware and delivers recurrent monitoring to the customer. The service integrates drone capture, station data, and a platform used for recurring review of the farm.

## Revenue Model

Recurring monitoring priced per hectare per year (deck cites about US$100/ha/year). Example packaged offer: about US$15k/year for 150 ha of Hass avocado, with AgroLens owning install, operation, and maintenance.

## Target Market

Agroexport producers and farm managers, starting with Hass avocado; deck validation also references mango, blueberry, and other export crops. Deck cites 18 clients and about US$270k ARR as an early scale signal within a larger Peru SAM narrative.

## Demand Evidence

Deck validation themes: high share of agroexport stakeholders treat monitoring as a relevant problem; stated willingness to pay above AgroLens pricing in surveyed profiles; preference for service vs. buying and operating hardware alone.

## Competitors

Alternatives include manual scouting, satellite or ERP tools, and other ag monitoring providers; deck positions an integrated recurrent service vs. self-operated hardware.

Team capabilities referenced in deck span business and pricing models, computer vision, web platform, hardware integration with drones, and operational deployments across multiple farms.
