## Elevator Pitch

Modular Infrastructure 4.0 for rural Colombia: circular, secure perimeter systems built from recycled polymer composite posts with integrated solar energy, lighting, and sensors—replacing short-lived wood fencing.

## Climate Problem

Poorly managed plastic waste contaminates soils and water and emits CO₂ when burned or degraded; rural areas lack durable, low-carbon perimeter infrastructure.

## Solution

DANTIUM manufactures recycled polymer composite posts with autonomous solar PV, LED lighting, and presence sensors for agricultural and rural fencing—extreme durability (**+50 years** lifespan per deck) and active security.

## Revenue Model

Sales of fencing systems and modular perimeter infrastructure to medium and large livestock/agricultural producers; public–private projects and custom rural deployments.
