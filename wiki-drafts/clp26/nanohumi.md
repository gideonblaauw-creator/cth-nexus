## Elevator Pitch

Nano-Humi is a microencapsulated biofertilizer (solid 1 kg format) that regenerates soil, protects it, and increases yield—aimed at ecological and organic farmers in southern Peru.

## Climate Problem

Degraded soils, pesticide reliance, and loss of productivity push organic growers to seek proof before switching inputs; deck cites widespread pesticide exceedances in food and farmer belief that “the soil doesn’t yield like before.”

## Solution

Microencapsulated biocomplex applied within organic management to recover degraded soil without leaving organic protocols—positioned vs. urea and conventional biofertilizers.
