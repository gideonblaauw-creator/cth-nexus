## Elevator Pitch

ECO-BLOQUE **40/35/25** turns coastal and agricultural waste into commercial biomass blocks for industry. Deck composition: **35%** coconut fibre, **25%** sargassum, plus **40%** and bamboo fibre called out in the deck mix. Tagline: *«Transformando las crises costeras y agrícolas en energía térmica industrial.»* Blocks are positioned at roughly **25,000–30,000 BTU** for partial substitution of fossil fuels in thermal processes.

## Climate Problem

Deck baseline (Phase 1, five companies): **840,000 gal** of Bunker No. 6 burned per year, estimated **8,568 t CO₂**. Sargassum on coasts and coconut/agricultural residues are underutilized while industry relies on heavy fuel oil.

## Solution

Combine sargassum retrieval and valorization with coconut fibre (and bamboo fibre in the mix) into **ECO-BLOQUE 40/35/25** for co-firing in industrial boilers. Deck target: substitute **50%** of fuel with a **70/30** biomass blend (wording in deck; block product marketed as 40/35/25).

## Revenue Model

Two streams in deck: **(1)** service — sargassum retrieval and valorization for hotels and coastal zones; **(2)** product — sale of biomass blocks to industrial customers seeking partial fossil fuel substitution.

## Target Market

Deck Phase 1: five named industrial users (e.g. Laboratorio Farach, Plastifar, Helados Bon — thermal oil/gasoil volumes cited in deck). Expansion vision: Caribbean and Latin America where sargassum, coconut waste, and bamboo coexist with industrial heat demand.
