## Elevator Pitch

Clean Sim transforms clinical residues into eco-simulators: hyper-realistic surgical training tools that reuse material otherwise destined for incineration, while helping health professionals practice safely.

## Climate Problem

Healthcare generates residues with high disposal cost and environmental impact. Non-hazardous fractions can be reprocessed instead of incinerated, but reuse in training products is not yet standard.

## Solution

Bags-to-recycle flow: assessable molds, local production, and reprocessed material formed into skin kits and micro surgical simulators for B2B (hospitals, universities) and B2C (students and professionals).

## Revenue Model

Deck: create and sell eco-simulators — **USD 100** B2C / **USD 8K** B2B examples; hospitals pay for waste handling at lower cost than disposal while buying premium simulators. Deck cites **~50K USD/year** revenue context on cover slide (verify).
