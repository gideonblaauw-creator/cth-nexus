## Elevator Pitch

Circuito Cero is a circular logistics network from the Caribbean to industry: we connect the glass chain and transform overlooked glass waste into high-purity industrial raw material.

## Climate Problem

Colombia faces a large gap in glass packaging recovery for industrial supply and HORECA. Poor recovery drives unnecessary raw-material demand and emissions linked to waste and inefficient logistics. The deck cites a deficit on the order of **75k tons/year** (verify exact scope in deck).

## Solution

Intelligent circular logistics for glass: collection, sorting, and recovery that links recyclers and buyers so recovered glass feeds bottling and industrial users as high-purity input.

## Revenue Model

Revenue from sale of recovered glass as industrial raw material, logistics and recovery services, and circular-economy partnerships along the glass value chain. *(Deck financial slide not fully extracted — detail pending.)*
