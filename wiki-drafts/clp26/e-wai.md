## Elevator Pitch

Hydrological intelligence, decisions on time: AI water-monitoring platform that turns hydrological data into environmental compliance reports for consultants and accredited labs.

## Climate Problem

Manual transformation of monitoring data into compliance reports is slow and error-prone; fear of fines drives demand for faster, reliable reporting on water obligations.

## Solution

Platform ingests universal hydrological datasets, maps anomalies, and auto-generates normative compliance reports—deck contrasts **3–5 days** manual work vs **~20 minutes** with e.WAI and near-zero manual errors.
