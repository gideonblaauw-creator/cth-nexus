## Elevator Pitch

Biochar from coconut shell—biotechnology to regenerate agriculture in Central America and beyond. Nutrichar sells premium nano-biochar and field IoT monitoring to specialty coffee and high-value crop producers.

## Climate Problem

Soil degradation and drought stress reduce productivity for specialty coffee and cacao. Coconut biomass waste is abundant; deck positions biochar and soil regeneration to retain moisture and reduce reliance on imported chemicals.

## Solution

Nutrichar Premium (PaaS): about 1 tonne of nano-biochar plus an IoT monitoring station in the field (deck cites ~$1,800 USD/TM package framing). High-porosity living substrates to optimize irrigation and root protection in dry periods.

## Revenue Model

Direct sales of nano-biochar and monitoring packages to specialty coffee and cacao producers and cooperatives; deck references scaling tonnage scenarios (e.g., SAM/TAM tonnage and USD/year figures in deck excerpts).

## Target Market

Specialty coffee growers and cacao producers in El Salvador (beachhead), cooperatives seeking collective productivity gains, and protected agriculture / greenhouse users per deck segmentation.
