## Elevator Pitch

Conservation that pays for itself. Siloe Garden combines cloud-forest ecotourism, education, and conservation at La Cangreja, El Guarco, Cartago (1,600 m.a.s.l.), with a native plant nursery serving families, students, and garden lovers.

## Climate Problem

Cloud forest and watershed habitat need ongoing protection. Without revenue tied to conservation, land use pressure continues. Siloe Garden targets making forest stewardship economically viable on site.

## Solution

Interpretive cloud-forest trails (stations covering water, flora, birdwatching, medicinal plants, pollinators, soil, and restoration), guided tours, student workshops, picnic areas, and a ~120 m² nursery producing medicinal, fruit, and ornamental plants— with organic composting and no agrochemicals on the protected hectare.

## Revenue Model

Ecotourism visits (deck cites a single tariff band of about $3–$5, same for nationals and foreigners), nursery plant sales (deck cites roughly $3.5–$5.5 per plant context), and related on-site experiences. Deck cites ~80–200 plants per month from the nursery.

## Target Market

Primary and secondary schools (ecology tours and workshops; deck target 200+ students/year), families in Cartago and the GAM (~20–45 minutes away), and plant and garden lovers (medicinal and ornamental plants). Rural tourism in Cartago province cited at +8%/year in deck materials.
